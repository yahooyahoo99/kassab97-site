Privacy policy

Gold Backups is used only by its owner.

  • It requests the Google Drive scope drive.file, which only allows access to files the tool itself creates.
  • It uploads backups that are encrypted on the owner's server before upload. It does not read, list or modify any other Drive files.
  • No data is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account page under "Third-party apps with account access".
